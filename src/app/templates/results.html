Method

Each score sheet becomes a number from 0 to 100 using the organizer's weights. Then every judge's sheets are compared with their own average and spread (pulled toward the event average when a judge scored only a few projects), and placed back on the event-wide scale. A project's score is the average of its corrected sheets. The full derivation is in JUDGING.md in the source repository.
